# Credit System (Pro)

> **Pro feature** - Requires WP Career Board Pro.

You can charge employers credits to post jobs and to feature them. Employers buy credits and track their balance from their dashboard.

Open the settings at **Career Board → Settings → Credits**. Set a job's credit cost per board under **Career Board → Settings → Boards**.

Credits can be sold through Stripe, PayPal, WooCommerce, Paid Memberships Pro or MemberPress. WP Career Board does not process payments itself.

The full guide is in the Pro documentation:

- **Credit system - overview** - how credits are held, spent and refunded, and the setup checklist.
- **Credit packages and costs** - prices and packs.
- **Direct payment gateways (Stripe and PayPal)**, **Selling credits through WooCommerce**, **Granting credits with Paid Memberships Pro** and **Granting credits with MemberPress** - one guide per way of selling credits.
- **Manual Credit Adjustments & Refunds** - correct a balance by hand.

Employers can read how their balance works in [Employer credit balance](../for-employers/10-employer-credit-balance.md).
