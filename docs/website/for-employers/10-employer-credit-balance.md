# Your credit balance

> **Pro feature** - the Credits tab, buying credits and receipts come with WP Career Board Pro.

If your site charges credits to post jobs, you can see your balance in the Employer Dashboard, buy more credits and feature a job. The site owner sets the prices. Setup is in [the admin credit guide](../admin-guide/06-credit-system.md).

## See your balance

When credits are on, the sidebar shows a **Credits** item with your balance as its badge. With Pro, click it to open the Credits tab. Without Pro the sidebar shows only the balance.

## How credits are spent

- Credits are held when you submit a job to a board that charges.
- They are spent when the job goes live.
- They come back if the job does not go live, for example when a moderator rejects it.

If your balance is too short, the job is not created. You see "Insufficient credits. This board requires N credits but your balance is M." and a **Buy credits** button that opens the Credits tab.

If a moderator approves your job while your balance is too short, the job stays Pending and shows **Awaiting payment** in My Jobs until your balance covers it.

## Featured job upgrades

If your site sells featured placement, you can spend credits to make a job list first. There are two ways:

- **When you post** - on the last step of the job form, tick "Feature this job: it lists first for N days (X credits)". If the featuring charge fails, the job is still posted and you are told why it was not featured.
- **Later** - click **Feature** on a live job in **My Jobs**. You confirm the cost ("Featured jobs list first. This uses N credits from your balance.") and the job gets a **Featured** badge.

The checkbox and the **Feature** button appear only when the site prices featuring. Featured status ends after the length of time the site owner set (30 days by default). The job stays live and only loses the boost. Click **Feature** again to renew it.
