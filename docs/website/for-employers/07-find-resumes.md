# Find resumes

> **Pro feature** - requires WP Career Board Pro.

You can search the public resumes of candidates without waiting for them to apply.

## What Pro adds

- A **Find Candidates** page. The setup wizard creates it at `/find-candidates/`.
- Search and filters for skills, experience, location and candidates who are open to work.
- A candidate only appears when they have turned on the **Public** toggle on their resume.
- The site owner chooses who can browse the directory: everyone, logged-in members only, or approved employers only.
- A **Resume Map** block that pins public resumes on a map.

## Save resumes

Resumes you save appear under **Saved > Saved Resumes** in your Employer Dashboard.
