# Review applications

You can review the applicants for each of your jobs in a List or a Board (Kanban) view, change each applicant's status, rate them and keep private notes for your team.

## Open applications

1. Open the **Employer Dashboard**.
2. Click **Applications** in the sidebar (under Hiring). The badge shows your total applicant count.
3. Pick a job in the job selector at the top. Until you pick one, the view asks you to select a job.

## List view and board view

A **List / Board** toggle sits above the applicants.

- **List** - applicants on the left, and the selected applicant's cover letter, application answers, resume and status on the right.
- **Board** - a Kanban board with one column per status: Submitted, Reviewing, Shortlisted, Hired and Rejected. Drag a card to another column, or use the **Move to** menu on the card, to change the status. Press Enter or Space (or click) on a card to open the applicant's details. A note under the board counts applications that are not on it (closed, withdrawn or removed).

Both views are in the free plugin.

## What you see for each applicant

- The applicant's name, with initials
- Their email address (in the detail panel)
- The job they applied to
- The date they applied
- Their current status

## Filter by status

The pills above the list narrow the applicants for the selected job: **All**, **Submitted**, **Reviewing**, **Shortlisted**, **Rejected** and **Hired**. Each pill shows a live count for the whole job, not just the applicants loaded so far.

The dashboard loads 50 applicants at a time. Click **Load more applicants** at the end of the list for the next 50.

## Application statuses

| Status | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | The application arrived and you have not reviewed it |
| **Reviewing** | You are looking at this candidate |
| **Shortlisted** | The candidate is worth moving forward |
| **Rejected** | You are no longer considering this applicant |
| **Hired** | The offer was accepted and the position is filled |
| **Withdrawn** | The candidate withdrew. Read-only: you cannot change it |
| **Closed** | You closed the job before deciding on this applicant. Read-only |

Candidates see **Rejected** as "Not selected" and **Closed** as "Position closed". Withdrawn, Closed and removed-job applications are final. Only reopening the job gives Closed applicants back their earlier status.

## Change an applicant's status

In **List view**, use the status control in the detail panel. In **Board view**, drag the card to another column or pick a status in its **Move to** menu. The change saves at once with no page reload, and the candidate is notified ("Status updated. The candidate has been notified."). Setting the status the applicant already has changes nothing and sends nothing ("No change. The candidate was not notified.").

Setting **Rejected** sends the candidate a "not selected" email in place of the generic status email. Guests who applied with an email address are notified too. If an applicant left no email address, you see "Status updated. This applicant left no email address, so they were not notified."

## Rate applicants and keep notes

Open an applicant and find **Your rating and notes**. Click a star to rate from 1 to 5, and click the same star again to clear it. Add notes for your hiring team below the stars. You can delete your own notes. Only the job's employer and site staff see ratings and notes. Candidates never do, and they are removed if the application is later anonymised.

## Export applicants to CSV

Click **Export CSV** above the applicants to download the selected job's applicants as a spreadsheet. See [Applicant CSV Export](./09-csv-export.md).

## Rank applicants by AI fit (Pro)

With WP Career Board Pro and an AI provider set up, a **Rank by AI fit** button appears above the applicant list. It scores applicants against the job and shows an **AI fit** score on each one. Read the full guide in the Pro documentation page "AI features".

## Custom hiring stages (Pro)

With WP Career Board Pro you can define your own hiring stages, for example Screening, Interview and Offer. See [Application Pipeline](./06-application-pipeline.md).

## Read the resume

The detail panel shows the applicant's cover letter and their answers to your screening questions. When the applicant attached a resume, it shows **View Resume** and **Download Resume** links. Resume files are private: only the candidate, site staff and the employer the candidate applied to can open them.

## Contact applicants

Use the applicant's email address to reach out from your own mail client. The free plugin has no built-in messaging.

## When a candidate withdraws

You get an email, and the application stays in your list with a **Withdrawn** badge. You cannot change its status. The candidate can apply again later, which shows up as a new application.

## Who cannot apply

Employers do not see an Apply button on jobs, and nobody can apply to their own job.
