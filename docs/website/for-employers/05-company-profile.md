# Company profile

You can give candidates a public page about your company, with your open jobs listed on it.

## What the company page shows

- Company name, logo and tagline
- Website, LinkedIn and X / Twitter links
- An **About** section with your description
- **Company Details**: industry, company size, type, year founded and headquarters
- **Open Positions**: your jobs that are still taking applications

Candidates can click through from the page to any of your jobs.

## Set up your profile

1. Open the **Employer Dashboard**.
2. Click **Profile** in the sidebar (under Company).
3. Fill in your company details. A **Live Preview** pane shows how the page will look as you type.
4. Click **Save Profile**. You see "Profile saved successfully."

To see the published page, click **Public Page** in the sidebar. It opens your company page in a new tab.

## Profile fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Company Name | Yes | Shown as the page title |
| Company Logo | No | JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP. You can upload it after the profile is saved once |
| Tagline | No | A one-line description shown on your company page |
| About the Company | No | Rich-text description |
| Industry | No | Your industry |
| Company Size | No | 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1,000, 1,001-5,000 or 5,000+ employees |
| Company Type | No | Public Company, Privately Held, Self-Employed / Freelance, Non-profit, Government Agency, Educational Institution or Partnership |
| HQ Location | No | For example San Francisco, CA |
| Founded Year | No | A year, such as 2012 |
| Website | No | Shown as a link |
| LinkedIn | No | Your company LinkedIn URL |
| X (Twitter) | No | Your company X / Twitter URL |

Only **Company Name** is required. You can save with just the name and fill in the rest later.

## Your company page address

The address is built from your company name: `yourdomain.com/companies/your-company-name/`.

## Who can see it

Anyone can see your company page, with or without an account. The Company Archive block (the **Find Companies** page from the setup wizard) lets visitors browse all companies on the board.
