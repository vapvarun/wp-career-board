# Job feed

You can publish your live jobs as an XML feed at `/wcb-jobs.xml` and submit it to job aggregators. This needs WP Career Board Pro.

## What you can do

- Turn the feed on under **Career Board > Settings > Job Feed**. It is off until you do.
- Submit your feed URL to Indeed, or to another aggregator that accepts the same format.

## Where to read the full guide

See the WP Career Board Pro docs, page named **Job Feed**.
