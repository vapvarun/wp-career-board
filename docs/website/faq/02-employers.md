# FAQ for employers

## Why is my job not showing on the job board?

The most common reasons, in the order to check them:

1. It is **Pending**: the site holds new jobs for approval. My Jobs shows the status.
2. It shows **Awaiting payment**: a moderator approved it but your credit balance did not cover it. It goes live once you top up.
3. It has **Expired**: the deadline passed. Click **Reopen** for a new listing period.
4. It was **Rejected**: edit it and click **Resubmit**.

If none of these apply, ask your site admin to check whether the job was hidden by reports or a ban. See [Job missing from Find Jobs](../troubleshooting/02-jobs-and-applications.md#a-job-is-missing-from-find-jobs).

## Why did my job expire?

Every job runs until its deadline: the date you set, or the site's default listing length (30 days unless the owner changed it). At the deadline the job stops taking applications and leaves the listings within the hour. You get a **Job Ending Soon** email 3 days before and a **Job Expired** email afterwards. Its page stays up and says it has expired, so shared links keep working. Applications you already received stay in your dashboard. **Reopen** starts a new listing period. See [Manage Your Jobs](../for-employers/03-manage-jobs.md).

## How do credits work?

On sites that charge for postings (this needs WP Career Board Pro), posting a job costs credits. They are held when you submit, used when the job goes live, and returned if the job is rejected. Your balance and a **Buy credits** button are in the **Credits** tab of your dashboard. If your balance is too short the form tells you and offers the button. See [Your Credit Balance](../for-employers/10-employer-credit-balance.md).

## How do I export my applicants?

Open **Employer Dashboard → Applications**, select a job and click **Export CSV**. You get every applicant for that job, not just the 50 loaded on screen. See [Applicant CSV Export](../for-employers/09-csv-export.md).

## Can candidates see my notes and ratings?

No. Ratings (1 to 5 stars) and notes are visible only to the job's employer and site staff.

## What happens to applicants when I close a job?

Applicants you have not hired or rejected move to **Closed** and each gets one email saying the position is closed. Applicants you already decided on keep their status. Reopening the job gives the Closed applicants their earlier status back without emailing them again.

## Why can't I apply to a job as an employer?

A member who posts jobs cannot apply to jobs, and nobody can apply to their own job. Use a separate candidate account to test the apply flow.
