# FAQ for candidates

## Do I need an account to apply?

Usually not. Most sites let you apply with your name and email. Some sites turn on **Require login to apply**; then the job page shows **Sign in to apply**. To register, use the site's **Employer Registration** page and choose **Find a Job**.

## Why does my application say "Not selected" or "Position closed"?

**Not selected** means the employer chose not to move forward (employers see the same status as "Rejected"). **Position closed** means the employer closed the job before deciding, for example because the role was filled. You get an email in both cases. See [My Applications](../for-candidates/04-my-applications.md).

## Why can't I apply again to the same job?

While an application is open you can apply to a job only once. As a guest you also cannot apply to the same job with the same email within 24 hours. If you withdrew, you can apply again.

## Can I withdraw an application?

Yes, from **Candidate Dashboard → My Applications**, until the employer hires or declines you, unless the site turned withdrawing off. The application stays in your list as Withdrawn and the employer is emailed.

## I applied as a guest. How do I see my application?

Register with the same email you applied with. Your earlier guest applications move to the new account when it is created and appear in **My Applications**. If you already had an account when you applied as a guest, they stay under the guest email.

## Who can see my resume?

Resume files are private. Only you, the site staff and the employer of a job you applied to can open the file. Public resume pages are a Pro feature with their own visibility setting. See [Profile, Account & Notifications](../for-candidates/11-profile-and-account.md).

## How do I stop deadline reminder emails?

Untick them under **Candidate Dashboard → Settings → Email Notifications**. Emails about your account, applications and payments are always sent.

## How do I delete my account?

**Candidate Dashboard → Settings → Privacy & My Data → Delete my account**. Your account is locked at once and deleted after 14 days. Sign in again before then and choose **Keep my account** to cancel. Employers keep your applications with your details removed ("Deleted candidate").
