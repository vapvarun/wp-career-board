# AI troubleshooting

You can use this page to find out why an AI control is missing.

## The AI controls do not appear

- Check that WP Career Board Pro is active.
- Go to **Career Board > Settings > AI Settings** and confirm a provider and key are saved.
- The AI Chat Search block shows site owners a notice until a provider and valid key are set.

## Something else is wrong

See the WP Career Board Pro docs, page named **AI features**, for limits and provider behaviour.
